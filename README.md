# My favorite series *Vikings*
Type: Historical Drama TV Series

Creator: Michael Hirst

Seasons: 6 (Aired on the History Channel and later Prime Video)

Starring: Travis Fimmel (as Ragnar Lothbrok), Katheryn Winnick (as Lagertha), Clive Standen (as Rollo), and Alex Høgh Andersen (as Ivar the Boneless), among others.

Plot: The series begins in the 8th century and largely follows the sagas of the legendary Viking chief Ragnar Lothbrok, his family, and his descendants as they rise to power and launch major raids and explorations across Europe.

Follow-up/Spin-off: A sequel series, Vikings: Valhalla, is set 100 years after the original, featuring new characters like Leif Erikson, Freydís Eiríksdóttir, and Harald Hardrada.
